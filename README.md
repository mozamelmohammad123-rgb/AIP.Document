# Seneca Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose

To maintain high standards of academic integrity across the Seneca community and provide the foundation for research, teaching, learning, and working practices.

## Scope

This policy applies to **current students** and **employees**.

## Key Definitions

### Academic Integrity

> A commitment, even in the face of adversity, to six fundamental values: **honesty**, **trust**, **fairness**, **respect**, **responsibility**, and **courage**. These values enable academic communities to translate ideals into action, ensuring credentials accurately reflect demonstrated knowledge and abilities.

### Core Violations

- **Cheating:** Obtaining credit through dishonest or deceptive means, including plagiarism, impersonation, falsification, and unauthorized collaboration
- **Plagiarism:** Using another's work without proper attribution
- **Self-Plagiarism:** Unauthorized reuse of one's own previous work
- **Contract Cheating:** Submitting work created by a third party as one's own
- **Unauthorized Aid:** Using non-approved supports to complete coursework or assessments
- **Undeclared AI Use:** Using artificial intelligence without disclosure

## Policy

### 1. General Principles

- Seneca upholds a learning community that values **academic integrity**, **honesty**, **fairness**, **trust**, **respect**, **responsibility**, and **courage**
- Commitment to delivering high-quality education while supporting an **equitable** and **inclusive** learning environment

### 2. Academic Integrity Program

- **Fundamental purpose:** Ensure academic credentials accurately demonstrate student knowledge and learning
- Requires alignment of policy, procedure, faculty development, and student programming
- Grounded in institutional philosophies on academic integrity

### 3. Responsibilities

- **Seneca's role:** Provide information and resources to promote clear understanding of academic integrity
- **Student's role:** Inform faculty of circumstances affecting academic performance; can report suspected violations
- **Reporting:** Students can report violations following established procedures

### 4. Sanctions

- Applied according to violation severity
- Student record consulted for mitigating or aggravating factors
- Violations may be escalated to Student Code of Conduct procedures if applicable

## Academic Integrity Procedures - Information Gathering

| Action | Timeline | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| Report violation to school designate | As soon as possible | Faculty, invigilator, student, support staff |
| Inform student and discuss informally | Within reasonable timeframe | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
| Collect and retain assessment materials | Within 5 days | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
| Notify student (no violation found) | Within 2 Seneca business days | Faculty |
| Notify student and commence formal process | Within 5 Seneca business days | Faculty |
